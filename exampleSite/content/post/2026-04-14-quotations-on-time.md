Two passages I want to keep close this Eastertide. The first from George
Herbert's *The Country Parson*, the second from Rowan Williams.

> Time is the rule of God's mercy, by which he giveth, and taketh away.

And:

> Patience is not resignation, but the willingness to remain in the place where
> God is teaching us. <cite>— Williams, *Christ on Trial*</cite>
